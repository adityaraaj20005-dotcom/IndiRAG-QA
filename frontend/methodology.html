06 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY

Nine stages from raw PDF to validated citation.

IndiRAG-QA combines deterministic lexical retrieval, multilingual dense embeddings, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion with strict answerability gating.

System Architecture

PYMUPDF · BM25 · TF-IDF · SENTENCE TRANSFORMERS · RRF · GEMINI
01

Document processing

Ingests single PDFs or nested collections with deterministic document IDs, folder metadata, and deduplication.

02

Text extraction and segmentation

Uses PyMuPDF to extract page-level Unicode text (Latin & Devanagari) and segments it into overlapping passage chunks with page anchors.

03

BM25 and TF-IDF

Captures exact lexical matches for policy codes, numeric limits, and proper nouns that dense vectors can smooth over.

04

Multilingual sentence embeddings

Encodes queries and passages via Sentence Transformers into a shared cross-lingual semantic space across English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

05

Hybrid retrieval using Reciprocal Rank Fusion

Fuses BM25, TF-IDF, and dense rankings using RRF (1 / (k + rank)) without fragile raw-score normalization.

06

Gemini-powered RAG

Conditions Gemini on the top-K fused passages with strict grounding instructions to prevent parametric-memory fabrication.

07

Answerability detection

Classifies each query as ANSWERABLE or UNANSWERABLE when retrieved passages lack sufficient evidence.

08

Citation validation

Maps generated claims back to verified source filenames, page numbers, and passage identifiers returned by the retriever.

09

Research evaluation

Evaluates Recall@K, Precision@K, Hit Rate@K, MRR, Exact Match, Token F1, and Direct-LLM vs. RAG ablation on annotated datasets.

SCIENTIFIC TRANSPARENCY

Current Research Limitations

1. Scanned & Image-Only PDFs

PyMuPDF extracts embedded text layers; scanned Devanagari PDFs without OCR require an external OCR pre-processing pass.

2. Uncalibrated Confidence

Raw retrieval and LLM logprobs are not yet Platt-scaled; confidence is explicitly reported as unavailable when uncalibrated.

3. Informal Hinglish Spelling

Highly non-standard Romanized Hindi spelling variants can reduce lexical BM25 overlap, increasing reliance on dense embeddings.

4. Multi-Hop Synthesis

Single-pass Top-K retrieval may miss evidence split across distant tables or appendices without iterative query decomposition.
