CROSS-LINGUAL / 26

Not a black-box chatbot. Every answer is anchored to verifiable document passages, page numbers, and explicit answerability detection.

IndiRAG-QA bridges the language gap between official English institutional documents and everyday Hindi or code-mixed Hinglish questions. Upload PDFs once, retrieve across lexical and multilingual semantic indices, and inspect every citation before trusting a single claim.

01

Upload knowledge.

Ingest multi-page PDFs and organized research folders via PyMuPDF extraction and overlapping passage segmentation.

02

Retrieve meaning.

Combine BM25, TF-IDF, and multilingual sentence embeddings with Reciprocal Rank Fusion to surface relevant evidence across scripts.

03

Generate with evidence.

Synthesize grounded answers with Gemini, validate passage citations, and flag unanswerable queries instead of hallucinating.

CROSS-LINGUAL RETRIEVAL

Query in Hinglish, Hindi, or English—and trace every claim back to the exact page.

Code-mixed queries break keyword-only search. IndiRAG-QA pairs lexical precision (BM25 & TF-IDF) with multilingual dense embeddings so a Hinglish question retrieves the right English or Hindi clause.
